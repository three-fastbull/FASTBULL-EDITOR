# Delivery quality gates

The pipeline must pass `quality_report.json`: non-empty MP4, H.264 video, audio present, 1080x1920 vertical output, expected duration, and caption data.

Before client delivery, a person must also:

1. Watch the full clip and confirm no cut clips a Thai syllable or changes meaning.
2. Verify spelling, names, numbers, product terms, subtitles, factual claims, and offer wording.
3. Check headline readability, face clearance, caption safe zone, insert relevance, and CTA timing on a phone-sized preview.
4. Confirm every local B-roll asset is client-owned, licensed, public domain, or explicitly approved.
5. Listen on headphones and phone speakers; reject distortion, distracting SFX, or abrupt audio joins.

If any manual item is unverified, describe the output as a review draft rather than client-ready.
